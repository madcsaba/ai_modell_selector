Meme corner

Method. Fit is a weighted average of the editorial rating (1–5) for the selected needs and the monthly cost; the “What matters more?” slider sets the weight. Ratings are estimates to help you orient, not measurements. The writing rating was made for Hungarian text.

Providers do not publish energy use per request, so this page does not estimate energy. A smaller, cheaper model usually means less computation too, but that is not an exact measure. The petition for an AI energy label asks the industry to close this gap.

Sources. Prices, context sizes and release dates come from the open models.dev database as of 16 November 2026 and from the providers’ documentation. An analysis of the 1,400+ models available in OpenCode is on the OpenCode models page (in Hungarian).
